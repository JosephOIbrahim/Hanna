### §3.1 First-principles — §12.6 channel contradiction (lead)

The blueprint's own 2026-05-20 audit names the tension at `HANNA_BLUEPRINT.md` Audit Log finding #4 (lines 27–28): *"'Always-on producer' contradicts MCP-tools-only. MCP tools fire only when Joe opens a Claude session. A producer that only speaks when spoken to is a logbook."* The committed channel (browser-rendered brief) is also flagged as "the wrong landing zone for a creative director who lives in Houdini and on iPhone."

Evidence at HEAD that the contradiction is unresolved:

- **`README.md:7`** opens with the claim: *"Always-on AI producer for a creative portfolio. Surfaces decisions; does not make them."* This is the front-door framing of the project. The reader's first sentence is the contradiction.
- **`scripts/first_hanna_brief.py:128`** prints to stdout and **line 127** persists to SQLite. The brief lands in two places Joe must explicitly open to read.
- **`bin/hanna-brief.command:36`** runs `open "$BRIEF_PATH"` on a static `web/templates/morning_brief.html` mockup — Phase-1 design reference per `HANNA_BLUEPRINT.md:213`, not a live brief delivery channel. The launcher comment at lines 9–10 acknowledges this: *"Phase 2 (post day-zero): swaps to running the python script that generates a fresh brief from live Harlo state, then opens the rendered result."*
- **`HANNA_BLUEPRINT.md:372`** parks the channel question as audit decision §12.6: candidates are browser, iMessage via Shortcuts, `osascript display notification`, menubar app, Calendar event auto-creation, Houdini shelf tool. The proposed default is *"one-day channel test — hand-author three days of fake briefs, ship via the cheapest channel(s), observe which Joe actually acts on vs. swipes."* The test has not run.
- **`HANNA_BLUEPRINT.md:213`** itself flags the constraint: *"the 920px asymmetric-gutter kit does not [transfer to non-browser channels]."* The Phase-1 mockup is design *posture*, not destination.

**Impact:** high. The contradiction is foundational. Every downstream lane (mcp_tools, stage, day_zero long-form, brief composer voice) is sized to the wrong audience-engagement model until the channel question lands. Building the rest of the substrate before resolving this is building a logbook with high production values.

**Confidence:** high. The tension is acknowledged in the blueprint itself (Audit Log finding #4 and §12.6); the un-run test is the explicit gating artifact.

**Recommendation:** open `D006` to author the one-day-channel-test plan (D-format per `docs/DECISIONS.md` lines 15–30) and ship the test before any further lane work. The test's cost is ~3 working days of hand-authored briefs across 2–3 channels; the unblock is every downstream design decision. See §5 for the concrete D006 shape.
